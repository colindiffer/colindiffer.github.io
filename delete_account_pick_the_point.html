Delete your Pick the Point data

Pick the Point does not require or create a user account. Your levels, coins, boosters, achievements, bonus-game progress, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Reset progress in the app

  1. Open Pick the Point.
  2. Tap the Settings button on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Reset beside Reset progress.
  4. Confirm the reset.

This clears unlocked and completed levels, coins, boosters, achievements, and bonus-game progress. Appearance, sound, and haptic preferences remain so the app keeps your chosen settings. A purchased “No Ads” upgrade is also kept, because it was paid for.

Delete all local app data

To remove all Pick the Point data, including preferences, uninstall the app. On Android you can alternatively use your device’s app settings to clear Pick the Point’s storage.

No server account to delete

Pick the Point has no sign-in, cloud save, or server-side game profile. There is therefore no Pick the Point account data held by DifferApps that needs a separate deletion request.

Analytics and advertising providers may retain information under their own policies. This information is not stored as a Pick the Point account. You can reset or limit advertising identifiers through your device settings.

On iOS, achievements and leaderboard scores sent to Apple Game Center are held by Apple against your Game Center profile. You can manage or delete that data through Apple (Settings → Game Center, or Apple’s Data and Privacy page). On Android, achievements and leaderboard scores sent to Google Play Games are held by Google against your Play Games profile. You can manage or delete that data in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings) or through your Google Account. Purchase history is held by Apple or Google.
