Dogogram

Logic puzzles with a pack of dogs.

Dogogram is a calm, clever nonogram game. The numbers beside each row and column tell you how many dogs sit together. Work out where every dog belongs and watch the pack wag, pant and hop when you get it right.

1,000 puzzles, 19 breeds and a fresh daily challenge. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone.
