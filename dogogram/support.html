Support

Stuck, found a bug, or want your data deleted? Email support@differapps.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

Common questions

How do I change my leaderboard name? Settings → Leaderboard name (2–16 characters).

How do I start again? Settings → Reset all progress (tap twice).

How do I change my ad or privacy choices? Settings → Privacy & ads.

How do I delete my data? Email us your leaderboard name and we'll delete your leaderboard entry, cloud save and anonymous ID.

Reporting a leaderboard name: email us with the name and we'll remove it.
