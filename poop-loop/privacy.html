Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Poop Loop is made by DifferApps. This explains what the app collects and why. There are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email address, location or contacts.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, boosters, settings and daily-gift streak are saved on your device only. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

Poop Loop shows ads from Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from ads and Google's privacy policy.

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iPhone, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment details and we never see them. The app only receives confirmation that a purchase completed.

What we don't do

Poop Loop does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, does not create accounts and does not sell your data.

Children

Poop Loop is not designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK and EU GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. We hold no personal data about you ourselves; for data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above.
