Poop Loop

Sort it before you clog it.

A cheeky, colourful sorting puzzle. Tap a tube, watch the cuties roll along the pipe, and fill the tanks to flush them clean. Plan your order or the pipe clogs.

Coming soon to iPhone, iPad and AndroidSupport

How it plays

  1. Tap a tubeIts next two pieces drop onto the pipe.
  2. Ride to a tankEach piece takes a tank holding its colour, or an empty one.
  3. Flush and winA full tank flushes clean. Empty every tube and tank to clear the level.

The catch: the pipe only holds so many pieces at once, so watch the LOAD meter. There is no timer, so take your time.

Poop Loop is free to play. It shows ads and offers optional coin packs as in-app purchases. You never need to buy anything to play.

FAQ

Is Poop Loop free?

Yes. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases for coin packs, which you can use to buy extra boosters.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

There are no accounts. Your progress is saved on your device. Ads and purchases need an internet connection.

Can I get my purchases back on a new phone?

Your progress is stored on your device, and coin packs are consumable purchases, so coins cannot be restored on a different device. If a purchase did not arrive, open the Store and tap “Restore purchases”, or email us.

The pipe clogged. What happened?

The pipe holds a limited number of pieces. Each tap drops two, and pieces with no free tank keep circling. If more arrive than fit, it clogs. Try a different tap order, or use the Plunger or Flush booster.
